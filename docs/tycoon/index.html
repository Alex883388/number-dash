Tycoon Town

Pick a city, open shops on its living map, choose the best spots, beat your rivals, spot the scams, and grow your company from a lemonade stand to an international airport.

💾 Back up or move your game

Your game saves by itself on this device after every move. A save code or file keeps a copy you can load on another device, or after clearing the browser.

❓ How to play

The whole city is one big map: drag to look around, pinch to zoom, tap a lot. Pick a business in Invest and choose where it goes: a business sells more in a good spot (a lemonade stand by the park, bubble tea near a school). Time runs by itself, a month a day: watch the money pop up over your businesses every hour. ⏸ pauses, 1× 2× 4× changes the speed, Next month jumps to the evening. The city fills up as others buy land, and rivals open next to you. Each business brings business cases: decide what a smart CEO would do, and watch out for traps and scams! Grow your net worth to open the next district, all the way to the Gateway with its airport and port. The seasons change the city, and now and then something happens on the map: roadworks, a carnival parade, a storm, a flood, an earthquake. Insurance costs a little every month and pays most of the repairs.
