💾 Back up or move your game

Your game saves by itself on this device after every move. A save code or file keeps a copy you can load on another device, or after clearing the browser.

❓ How to play

Pick a business you can afford and open it on a free plot. Every month (tap Next month) your businesses earn money, or sometimes lose it. Each business brings business cases: real problems where you decide what to do. Smart choices make a business earn more, steadier, or trusted. Watch out for traps and scams! Grow your net worth to open new districts: High Street, Downtown, then Skyline & Harbour.
