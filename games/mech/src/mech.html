❓ How to play
  • Move left and right: drag your finger across the screen, or hold ◀ ▶. Jump with the Jump button (or flick up).
  • Your guns fire straight ahead by themselves when a robot is in front of you: line up with it to hit it.
  • Gates block the road. Read the question, stand under the panel with the right answer (it lights up gold) and press the gold Answer button.
  • Red balls: move aside. Orange waves along the ground: jump. Red lanes and circles on the road: get out of them!
  • Finish levels to unlock new weapons. Every tenth level has a boss: stand under the right answer orb and press Answer to break its shield, then blast it.
  • Keyboard: ← → move, ↑ or Space jump, F or Enter answer, 1–7 weapons, N nova, P pause.
  • ⭐ Finish the level. ⭐⭐ Without your mech going down. ⭐⭐⭐ And every answer right first time.
