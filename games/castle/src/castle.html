How to play

  • 🏰 Zombies walk to your castle in waves. If its health runs out, the level is lost.
  • 🕹️ Drag the left side of the screen to walk (or W A S D and the arrow keys). Your weapon fires by itself at the nearest zombie.
  • 🪓 Walk into trees for 🪵 wood and into rocks for 🪨 stone. Zombies drop 🪙 gold.
  • 🔨 Build towers and barricades before each wave. Stand next to a tower to upgrade it with gold.
  • 🔫 Switch weapons for each kind of zombie: armour, ghosts, ice and fire each have a weakness.
  • 👫 Play with friends: one hosts and shares the code, the others join on their own devices.
