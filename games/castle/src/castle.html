How to play

  • 🧟 Zombies walk the roads to your castle, round after round. Every zombie that gets in costs the castle lives (❤️). Hold out for 100 rounds, then keep going!
  • 🔨 Build towers beside the roads with your coins (🪙). Barricades and spike traps go on the road itself.
  • ⬆️ Tap a tower to upgrade it. Each tower has two paths: one path can go all the way to step 3, the other then stops at step 1. You can also change what it aims at, or sell it.
  • 🔫 Your weapon fires by itself. It levels up from the zombies you finish off, and every 5 levels it becomes something new.
  • 🕹️ Drag the left part of the screen to walk (or W A S D and the arrow keys). Walk into trees and rocks for a few coins and more room to build.
  • 🛡️ Learn the zombies: knights need bombs, fire or magic; shadows need a Ninja or a tower that can see them; ghosts and bats fly over barricades.
  • ▶ Speed the game up with the 1× button. 👫 Play with friends: one hosts and shares the code.
