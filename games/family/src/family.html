How to play

  • 🧟 Zombies have taken over Vietnam! Walk right along the street. When zombies come, the screen stops until they are all beaten.
  • 👊 Punch three times in a row for a combo: the third blow knocks zombies over. Jump and punch in the air for a flying kick (that is how to reach the bats).
  • ⭐ Hitting zombies fills your energy. A full bar lets you do your special: Bee's iPad spin, Bo's belly bounce, Bố's golf cart dash and Mẹ's slipper storm.
  • ❗ A zombie with a yellow ! is about to bite: step away, or hit it first. Two zombies bite at a time; the others wait their turn.
  • 🥁 A gate blocks the street with a question. Read it at the top, then smash the drum with the right answer. A wrong drum lets out two zombies and shows a tip.
  • 👑 Every place ends with a boss. Twice in the fight its question shield comes up: smash the right gong to break it, and the boss is dizzy for a moment.
  • 🥖 Bánh mì and phở make you strong again, chè fills energy, and coins add to your score. Smash crates and stalls to find them.
  • ❤️ Knocked out? A teammate standing next to you helps you up. Otherwise a family heart does (three each place).
  • 🎮 Touch: drag on the left to walk, buttons on the right. Keyboard: W A S D, J punch, K jump, L special; a second player uses the arrow keys with 1, 2, 3. Controllers: stick, A punch, B jump, X special.
