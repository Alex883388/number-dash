How to play

Jump

Tap, click, or press Space. Hold it down to keep hopping.

Rocket

After a pink portal you fly. Hold to rise, let go to glide down.

Rings and pads

Yellow pads bounce you on their own. Tap while touching a yellow ring for a jump in the air. Blue rings flip you upside down.

Math doors

A question pops up at the top. Each lane ends in a door with a number. Steer into the lane with the right answer.

Practice mode

Green diamonds save your spot so you can learn the tricky parts. Stars only count in a normal run.

Slow motion

Need more time for a sum? Press Shift, S or ↓, or tap the clock button. Everything moves at a third of the speed for one second. You get three per try.

Ball

An orange portal turns you into a ball. Tap to roll onto the ceiling, tap again to roll back down.

UFO

A purple portal gives you a flying saucer. Every tap is a little hop up, like flapping wings.

Wave

A light blue portal makes you a zig-zag dart. Hold to go up, let go to go down.

Mini and speed

A green portal makes you tiny (smaller jumps), an orange one makes you big again. Chevrons change the speed: one is slower, three is faster.

More rings and pads

Pink rings give a small hop, green rings flip you to the ceiling, purple pads launch you extra high.

Keys

Esc or P pauses. R restarts. M mutes the music.
